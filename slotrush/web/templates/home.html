Who got a seat

Eighty bots asked one 30-seat class for a place in the same instant. Without a lock, each read seats_left, paused 50 ms and wrote back its stale value minus one. With SELECT … FOR UPDATE they queued on the row. Pick a guard: on the left the seats, on the right the students the database let in.
